# Third-party 3D assets

All models in this directory are released under CC0 1.0. Original license files
are stored beside the imported files where the downloaded archive supplied one.

- `Quaternius/Bat.fbx`: LowPoly Animated Monsters by Quaternius.
  Source: https://opengameart.org/content/lowpoly-animated-monsters
- `Quaternius/Ghost.fbx` and `Atlas_Monsters.png`: Ultimate Monsters by Quaternius.
  Source: https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemonsters.html
- `Quaternius/Spider.fbx`: LowPoly Animated Easy Enemies by Quaternius.
  Source: https://quaternius.itch.io/animated-easy-enemies
- `KayKit/wall_doorway.obj`, `torch_mounted.obj`, and `dungeon_texture.png`:
  KayKit Dungeon Remastered by Kay Lousberg.
  Source: https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Dungeon-Remastered-1.0
- `QuaterniusKnight/*.fbx`: LowPoly Animated Knight by Quaternius.
  Source: https://opengameart.org/content/lowpoly-animated-knight
- `CuteMonsters/*.fbx` and textures: Cute Animated Monsters by Quaternius.
  Source: https://opengameart.org/content/textured-cute-monster-pack
- `KenneyGraveyard/*.fbx` and `colormap.png`: Graveyard Kit 5.0 by Kenney.
  Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/graveyard-kit

The project uses the FBX animation clips directly through Unity Playables. The
KayKit door frame and torch are static meshes; the door leaves, flame and light
remain game-driven so their existing opening and flicker behavior is preserved.
